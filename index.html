Chintzy is now MyICE

The app has a new name and new features: it now works out what a journey costs in a petrol or diesel car, compared with an electric car, as well as finding the cheapest petrol near you.

Open MyICE

Taking you there now. If you installed Chintzy on your home screen, open MyICE in your browser and install it again (Chrome: three dots, then Install app; Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen). You can then delete the old Chintzy icon. Your saved postcode and settings are carried over. If you had price-drop alerts on, MyICE switches them on again by itself.
