What works with no connection

Still works: opening the app, your saved postcode and settings, and the last list of stations you looked at (marked as a saved copy, with its age).

Needs internet: fresh prices, a new postcode, and price-drop alerts. The app must have been opened once online before it can open with no signal.

Price-drop alerts

How alerts work (and what stops them being annoying)

An alert looks like: Price drop at Tesco Andover, SP10 1UZ: was 139.9p, now 136.9p. Ranked 4th (was 7th).

  • Only real drops of at least your chosen size, at a station that is now in your top 10 for your fuel and distance.
  • At most one alert per check, at most 3 an hour and 6 a day.
  • The same station is not reported to you again for 2 hours.
  • None between 10 at night and 7 in the morning.
  • Your phone will ask once: Allow Chintzy to send notifications? Choose Allow.
  • The service keeps only the phone's private alert address, the position of your postcode rounded to about 1 km, your fuel and your settings.

About the prices

Prices come from the government Fuel Finder service (Open Government Licence) and are set by the stations themselves, so they can be a little behind the pump. Report a discrepancy. Chintzy has no adverts and no tracking. Companion to MyEV Calculator.
